FreshMart US Private Shelf: Packaging Artwork Guidelines (cereal and granola)

Our shoppers decide in seconds, so the front of every carton has to do the work.
The brand logo must appear on the front panel, and the product flavour or variant
name must be printed on the front panel in a size that is easy to read from the shelf.

Date marking matters to our store teams. The pack must tell the shopper where to find
the best before date, and the wording must use "Best Before" rather than "Use By" for
shelf-stable foods.

We ask every supplier to help shoppers recycle. Cartons must carry recycling guidance,
for example a How2Recycle label or a clear "recyclable" statement.

Storage instructions must be printed on the pack.

Any website, phone number or social handle printed on the pack must be real and owned
by the brand; leave them off if in doubt. We also expect a consumer contact route
(a phone number or website) on every pack so shoppers can reach the brand.

History: these guidelines were first written in 2019 after a supplier review.
