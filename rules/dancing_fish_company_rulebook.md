# Dancing Fish Granola Co. Labelling Standards (US)

> DEMO DOCUMENT. Written for the Esko AIForge prototype to show how a company
> rulebook sits on top of regulation. It is not a real Dancing Fish document.

Version 1.2. Applies to all retail cartons sold in the United States.

### DF-01 Approved copy must match word for word
Ingredient list, allergen statement, dietary statement, storage instructions and
marketing copy on the artwork must match the approved content sheet exactly.
Any difference in wording, order or percentages is a defect until the content
sheet is updated.

### DF-02 Nutrition values must match the approved content sheet
Every value in the Nutrition Facts panel must equal the per serving value on the
approved content sheet. If the content sheet itself is inconsistent, the label
team must resolve it before release.

### DF-03 Vegan claims
"Vegan", "Suitable for Vegans" and the Certified Vegan (vegan.org) mark may only
be used when no ingredient is of animal origin. Honey, milk, egg, gelatin,
beeswax, carmine and shellac are animal derived. A vegan product cannot declare
cholesterol above 0 mg.

### DF-04 Claims must come from the approved claims list
Every front or back panel claim (badges, flashes, certification marks) must
appear in the approved claims field of the content sheet. Claims not on that
list are not allowed.

### DF-05 Use US terminology on US packs
US packs use US nutrition terms (Calories, Sodium, Total Sugars). EU style
front-of-pack icons ("Saturates", "Salt", kJ energy) are not used on US packs,
and any front-of-pack nutrient numbers must equal the Nutrition Facts per
serving values.

### DF-06 No hidden objects in released artwork
Released artwork must not contain text or objects that are covered by other
artwork. Hidden content can reappear at the printer when layers are flattened
differently.

### DF-07 Precautionary allergen statements
"May contain" statements are only used for cross-contact risks. An allergen that
is an ingredient is never listed as "may contain". Tree nuts are always named
individually; the generic word "Nuts" is not used.

### DF-08 GMO wording
Use "Non-GMO" only with Non-GMO Project verification. The words "GMO free" are
not used on US packs. Flag for regulatory review.

### DF-09 Organic claims
The USDA Organic seal requires at least 95 percent organic ingredients. When the
seal is used, every organic ingredient is marked "Organic" in the ingredient
list, and the certifying agent is named below the manufacturer statement
("Certified organic by ...").

### DF-11 Folds and safe area
Copy must not cross a fold (crease) line, and must stay inside the safe area
marked on the dieline (the dashed artwork-panel line), so nothing is cut off,
split across two faces or lost in the glue area.

### DF-10 Dieline print marks
Dieline guide text ("Front panel", "Orientation", "Copy free") must be on a
non-printing layer and must not overlap live copy.
